About us

Not just a club, but a purposeful community anchored in three non-negotiable commitments:

Equity: A realization that any student has the potential to enroll, grow, and excel in computing. We are here to ensure that passion matters more than prior privilege, making sure every gateway into tech is wide open.

Service: Empowering our members to become ambassadors of the computer science discipline, helping underscore its importance as a mainstay in the school and community through peer mentorship, outreach, and collaborative problem-solving.

Excellence: A commitment to promoting outstanding scholarship in computer science coursework, where members challenge themselves academically and uphold the highest standards of technical craftsmanship.

A standing agreement among students who take this seriously that they will show up for each other — with code review, with interview prep, with the stubborn debugging session before a deadline.

What we actually do

We're just getting started. Right now, this is the one thing we do — more will get added here as the club grows.

Tech support for other clubs

Other Niles North clubs can come to us for anything tech — a website, a sign-up form, an internal tool, some automation, or just advice on how to build something. Submit a ticket and we'll match you with a member who can help code it, develop it, or just get you unstuck.

Who this is for

This is an honors society, which means membership means something. Here's what we ask for.

  • CourseworkCompleted or currently enrolled in at least one computer science course.
  • ApplicationShort written application, reviewed by current officers and the faculty advisor.
  • CommitmentAttendance at meetings and at least one mentorship or outreach activity per semester.
